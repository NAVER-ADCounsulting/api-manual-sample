1 What Are Performance DA?

Key message: Performance display ads (GFA) are display ads shown across Naver inventory in banner, video, and catalog formats, operated to drive conversion performance through targeting and AI optimization.

The Limits of SA and the Need for DA

  • Because Search Ads (SA) are based on search volume, there is a limit to how much advertisers can scale their volume.
  • Even if you capture demand for brand, generic, and competitor keywords, you cannot reach potential customers who are not searching.
  • DA reaches users who are not searching, playing the role of growing awareness and search volume itself.

The SA ↔ DA Virtuous Cycle

When running DA, you gain not only direct conversions but also increased awareness and search volume. This virtuous cycle, in which DA improves the volume and efficiency of SA, is the core of performance marketing.

Virtuous cycle 
SA (Search Ads)  ──  Revenue↑ + Reinvestment↑  ──▶  DA (Display Ads)
   ▲                                                      │
   │  Search volume↑ + CTR↑       Impressions↑ + Awareness↑│
   └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

2 Why Naver DA

A Growing Performance DA Market

  • While the mature SA market has seen somewhat slower growth, the performance DA market continues to trend upward and has greater growth potential.
  • Through steady ad-product improvements over the past six years, the influx of new advertisers and new inventory (placements) keeps increasing.

A Platform Where Content, Search, and Commerce Are All Connected

On Naver, content (blogs, Clip, webtoons) – search – commerce (Shopping, Pay, Membership) are all connected, making it a platform where the DA↔SA virtuous-cycle effect is maximized.

If DA is new to you, start with NDA (Naver Dynamic Ads). Using the Naver Shopping product database and user behavior data, it dynamically generates personalized ad creatives, so you can get started easily even without prior DA know-how.

4 Tips to Get the Most Out of NDA

  1. Use all products in your catalog in ads: The more products you have, the more you can build ads that account for diverse user needs. Note that if you have fewer than 4 products, the ad may not be shown at all.
  2. Use images that clearly show product characteristics: If similar images appear in succession, users may not recognize the differences between products, lowering the response rate. Use dedicated ad images when needed.
  3. Run ads continuously: The AI delivery logic becomes more refined the longer you run ads. Intermittent runs restart optimization each time, so continuous operation is recommended.
  4. Expand your ad audience pool: Run non-targeted, select "all available placements," and use the "target expansion feature" to reach potential customers with similar interests.

3 Types of Precise Retargeting Available Only on Naver ★

High-efficiency DA operations are possible using Store Targets, Customer Journey Targets, and Website Targets.

① Store Target — Customer Segments by Event

  • Targets customers who triggered a specific event at my store (period configurable from 1 to 180 days)
  • Event stages: Visit → Wishlist → Coupon issuance → Cart → Checkout start → Purchase complete
  • Deliver stage-appropriate messages through segmentation by event action
Success story: By raising the Store Target share to 47%, overall ad spend scaled up and ROAS rose 1.6x to a record of 250%

② Customer Journey Target — Brand-Centered Audience (Brand Store ONLY)

  • Includes not only my Brand Store visitors but also users who showed interest in or attempted to purchase my brand's products
  • Journey stages: Brand discovery → Brand interest → Purchase attempt → Purchase → Continued interest
  • Example: Drive conversions by showing benefit-focused creatives to hesitant customers in the "brand interest ~ just before purchase" segment
Success story (Desker Brand Week): Customer Journey Target CVR 3,319% (overwhelming compared to 421% for the purchase-intent target)

③ Website Target

  • Retargets users who triggered a specific event such as a visit or purchase on my website (own mall)
  • Can also be used by own-mall advertisers without a store

Custom (Keyword) Targeting — Naver ONLY

Targeting that reaches customers based on keyword searches, leveraging Korea's largest search data.

  1. Keyword selection: Select keywords with high impression OR conversion volume from your SA report
  2. Grouping: Classify by audience size and characteristics — a minimum target audience of 1,000 or more, classified by USP into own brand / competitor / generic (category)
  3. Creative alignment: Performance is best when creatives are set appropriately for each keyword target (e.g., capsule keyword → capsule creative)

6 Operating Strategies by Brand Awareness

Because the strengths and limits of SA differ by brand awareness, the role and objective of DA must be defined differently.

Category ① High-Awareness Commerce ② Low-Awareness Commerce ③ Non-Commerce (e.g., Lead Collection)
DA goalExpand market share & secure loyal customersAcquire new customers & expand brand search volumeAcquire new customers & expand brand search volume
Recommended campaign mixCatalog + ConversionTraffic / ConversionTraffic + Engagement
Recommended targetingRetargeting (Website·Store)Purchase intent / InterestsKeyword custom target
Recommended daily budget10x the conversion CPA10x the conversion CPA10x the conversion CPA

① High-Awareness Commerce Brands

  • Defending market share and managing customers are the top priority
  • Run the highly efficient NDA (Catalog Sales) as the first priority, centered on retargeting ads
  • When scaling, target new markets with purchase-intent/interest targets

② Low-Awareness Commerce Brands — Market Pioneers

  • With a small own-market size, expansion into new markets is essential. Early on, securing an ad audience pool in a short period matters more than efficiency
  • Strategy ① Gather an audience with traffic (purchase intent/interests) to expand catalog volume
  • Strategy ② Secure highly engaged users with conversion (keyword custom target) to expand catalog volume

③ Non-Commerce Brands (Finance, Insurance, Games, Legal, Clinics, etc.)

  • Often aimed at collecting leads and acquiring new users, with active push marketing among competitors
  • Since the target group is clear, actively use keyword custom targets, and we recommend also testing Engagement campaigns in parallel
  • Engagement ads: Seamless lead collection within the Naver environment via Naver profile auto-fill
Best Practice: The common success pattern is to build an audience with an initial traffic campaign, then expand volume by increasing the share of conversion and catalog. (Cosmetics C: ad spend expanded 580% YoY · ROAS improved 61% / Small appliances D: conversions ▲1,693%, etc.)
